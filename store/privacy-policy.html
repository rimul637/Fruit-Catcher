Fruit Catcher Privacy Policy

Effective November 22, 2026

This policy applies to Fruit Catcher for Android (com.fruitcatcher.fruit).

Information the game handles

Fruit Catcher works offline. It does not collect or share personal information and does not use accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Information stored on your device

The game stores your best score and your music and sound effects settings on your device. The game does not transmit these settings or the score. To delete them, clear the game’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the game.

Privacy questions

For questions about this policy, use the developer contact listed on the Fruit Catcher Google Play page.
